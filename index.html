Admin

Policies and information

Working drafts that describe how this page behaves today. They are not legal advice and need legal and accounting review before launch, including privacy law, HST, CRA reporting and marketplace-facilitator terms.

Privacy

Requests and offers are visible to everyone who can open this page. Support messages, newsletter sign-ups and partner applications are stored privately for the site owner and editors. The request form doesn’t collect phone numbers or emails.

Terms and conditions

Not yet written. A lawyer must draft them before launch, including marketplace-facilitator terms and rules for housing and vehicles. Seekr isn’t a real-estate brokerage or a motor-vehicle dealer.

Community guidelines

Describe items honestly, ask only for legal goods and services, treat other members with respect, and meet in public places for pickups.

Payment and escrow

Payments and escrow are not live. The planned design holds funds until pickup, delivery or service completion is verified. Release windows (24 or 48 hours) and inspection rules are pending approval and will be configurable by category.

Dispute resolution

Disputes are not live. The planned process pauses release, collects timestamped photos from both sides in a 12-hour evidence window, allows a settlement offer, then goes to manual review.
